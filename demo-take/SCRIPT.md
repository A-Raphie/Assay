### Scene 4: Proof, not promises (0:50–1:05)
**Criterion:** Data & Analysis + Trust
**Show:** The sha256 line on the card, then the strip: LIVE FEED · BINANCE MCP SERVER.
**Say:** "Every verdict carries the hash of the exact market response it judged. This one was judged seconds ago, off the live feed. Not a promise. A receipt you can audit."
**Action:** Cursor traces the hash. Slow move up to the strip. Hold.

### Scene 5: The desk (1:05–1:30)
**Criterion:** Product + UX
**Show:** /desk. Mode switch, scenario buttons, click Red day: HALT stamp in neutral ink. Point at clear + public link.
**Say:** "The desk is mission control. Five scenarios, one click each. Red day: the agent is down six percent, so every order halts. Pause, not panic. And every card carries a public link anyone can audit, no wallet, no login."
**Action:** Click Red day scenario. Hold on the HALT stamp. Point at the public link line.

### Scene 6: One prompt, two outcomes (1:30–1:45)
**Criterion:** Data & Analysis + Innovation
**Show:** Landing, ONE PROMPT TWO OUTCOMES section: 1,000.00 vs 0.00.
**Say:** "The whole pitch is these two numbers. Raw MCP: a thousand dollars out the door, zero checks. Through Assay: zero dollars out, and a card that says exactly why."
**Action:** Slow scroll through the section. Cursor over each number.

### Scene 7: Connect your agent (1:45–2:05)
**Criterion:** Agent OS centrality + Innovation
**Show:** CONNECT YOUR AGENT section, config snippet with both servers, copy button.
**Say:** "Connecting is real, not slides. Paste one config and your agent gets two servers: Binance to trade, Assay to check. The assay_check tool judges every order before spot dot new order runs. Blocked orders never reach the exchange."
**Action:** Click copy button. Cursor traces the config. Hold on the assay entry.

### Scene 8: Close (2:05–2:20)

**Show:** GitHub repo + live URL held on screen.
**Say:** "Assay: your agent places the trade, your rules make the call. The app, the source, and the check are on screen right now."
